# dan-assets (tf-image-hosting)

Public static asset hosting: `index.html` and files under `assets/`. Everything here
is public as soon as it is pushed.

## Rules
- Only add files that are meant to be public. No private images, personal documents,
  client material or anything with embedded metadata you haven't checked.
- Don't rename or delete existing assets; other pages and agents link to them by URL.

## Needs Dan's approval
Publishing new assets, deleting or renaming assets, hosting or remote changes, and
anything with privacy implications.
